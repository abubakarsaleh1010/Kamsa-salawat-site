Questions

Good to know

Where exactly does my donation go?

100% of donations go directly toward the expansion — materials, labor, and fittings. Stage updates are shared with the community as work progresses.

Can I give in instalments?

Yes. Many donors pledge a tier and pay in parts over a few months — just reach out to the building committee to arrange it.

Is there a way to dedicate my gift (sadaqah jariyah) to a loved one?

Yes — let us know when you give and we'll record it in the masjid's donor and dedication register.

Who oversees the funds?

The expansion committee, appointed by the masjid's board of trustees, oversees all funds and construction milestones.
